# StreamDrop iPhone Screenshot Plan

Use real app screens from the final TestFlight candidate. Do not show placeholder
content, loading skeletons, personal notifications, or debug UI.

## Recommended Sequence

### 1. Weekend Discovery

- Screen: Home with `This Weekend` and several movie cards visible.
- Caption: `Find your next weekend movie`
- Supporting line: `Recent streaming releases in one simple view`

### 2. Personal Filters

- Screen: Home with a filter panel open and selected language/service chips.
- Caption: `Filter the noise`
- Supporting line: `Choose your languages, services, genres, and release window`

### 3. Streaming Details

- Screen: Movie details with provider, date, genre, runtime, overview, and Save
  to Watchlist visible.
- Caption: `Know where to watch`
- Supporting line: `See streaming availability and movie details before saving`

### 4. Watchlist

- Screen: Watchlist containing at least three visually distinct movies.
- Caption: `Save it for later`
- Supporting line: `Keep a simple watchlist right on your iPhone`

### 5. Search

- Screen: Search results for a neutral, recognizable movie query.
- Caption: `Search beyond the weekend`
- Supporting line: `Find a movie and check its streaming information`

### 6. Friday Alerts

- Screen: Settings with Friday drop alerts enabled and Home Filters visible.
- Caption: `Make Friday movie night`
- Supporting line: `Get an optional reminder when the weekend begins`

## Capture Guidance

- Capture the largest required iPhone display size supported by App Store
  Connect, then reuse it for smaller accepted display classes where Apple
  permits.
- Keep all screenshots in portrait orientation.
- Use the same status-bar time and visual treatment across the set.
- Avoid claims such as “all releases,” “every service,” or guaranteed
  availability.
- Keep TMDB attribution visible in the Settings screenshot when practical.
- Do not use studio logos or marketing artwork outside what appears naturally in
  the app UI.
- Verify every shown provider and release date immediately before capture.

## Optional Caption Alternatives

- `Fresh streaming picks for the weekend`
- `Browse by language and service`
- `Movie details without the endless scroll`
- `Your weekend watchlist, simplified`
- `Friday reminders, only when you want them`
